BIO

THE ARTIST
IN MOTION

Tom Draco is a DJ focused on raw and hypnotic techno, characterized by dynamic sets where tempo and energy evolve constantly. His sessions function as a journey in perpetual transformation — moving between deep grooves, mental tension and moments of high intensity.

Influenced by the hypnotic tension of Oscar Mulero, the energetic narrative of Jeff Mills and the experimental sensibility of artists like Björk and The Chemical Brothers, his sound explores enveloping textures, BPM shifts and intense progressions designed to generate an immersive, emotional experience on the floor.

Oscar Mulero — Hypnotic Tension
Jeff Mills — Energetic Narrative
Björk — Experimental Sensibility
The Chemical Brothers — Emotional Architecture
