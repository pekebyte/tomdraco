Sound with intention.

I’m a music producer, composer and sound designer based in Madrid.

I work across a wide range of musical styles, from indie pop and alternative rock to electronic music, house and techno.

My approach to music production goes beyond sound. I’m interested in the creative process behind each project: developing concepts, shaping musical identities, exploring textures and translating ideas and emotions into fully realized compositions.

Working independently, I take projects from their earliest stages to their final form, combining songwriting, composition, arrangement, sound design, recording, vocal production, mixing and mastering.

My work is driven by curiosity and a constant interest in exploring different sonic languages. Whether building an atmospheric pop production, developing an electronic composition or creating a distinctive sound world from scratch, my focus remains the same: making music with character, intention and identity.

Solar

Conceptual electronic music projectFive chapters exploring migration, identity, excess, forgiveness, freedom and presence.

A journey from carrying the light, to rejecting it, to becoming it.

SOLAR is a five-chapter electronic music project exploring personal transformation through the experience of leaving South America and arriving in Europe. It uses that journey as the starting point for a deeper emotional narrative about identity, expectations, disorientation, excess, forgiveness and self-reconciliation.

Leaving →Arriving →Losing oneself →Becoming free →Staying

Reaching the place you once dreamed of does not necessarily mean finding peace. Transformation begins when you learn to reconcile with your past and embrace the present.

Explore the five chapters

01. DEJO / LLEVO – The Departure

An organic and atmospheric introduction built around Latin American percussion, memory and the decision to leave. Aviation-related sound design marks the transition into a new life.

02. 2 BIG – The Arrival

The excitement of a new environment becomes overwhelming. House evolves into darker, faster techno, translating overstimulation into musical progression.

03. FORGET / FORGIVE – The Darkness

House, techno and jazz explore nightlife, excess and emotional escapism. The recurring phrase introduces a turn from avoidance towards reconciliation.

04. FREE – Liberation

A house production with references to Spanish Holy Week processions: deep drums, snare rolls and brass. Different cultural identities integrate, making room to become someone new without abandoning the past.

05. AQUÍ / AHORA – Presence

Through repetition and a more peaceful atmosphere, the final chapter moves towards acceptance and embracing the present.

Concept, sound & credits

Musical direction

House, techno and experimental electronic elements meet organic Latin American percussion, electronic textures, synthesizer programming, jazz-influenced rhythms and Spanish brass and percussion. Dynamic transitions and evolving arrangements bring these influences into one musical journey.

Creative concept

The sun represents origin, memory and cultural identity, then becomes something the protagonist rejects. By the end, it is no longer something to carry or search for, but something that exists within. This progression informs the sound selection, arrangements and structure.

Each track contributes to a larger emotional and sonic narrative, where genres, instrumentation, sound design and lyrics evolve together. SOLAR explores how cultural identity can be transformed rather than replaced, and how music can communicate beyond words.

Landscapes
translated

Image, perception and soundThree sound compositions based on images by photographer Jon Cazenave.

What happens when an image stops being looked at and begins to be heard?

Each image is approached as a visual score: a field of colours, textures, densities, shapes and spatial relationships interpreted through sound. Reading the images from left to right, I translate their visual elements into sonic behaviours, exploring how movement, rhythm, texture and space can emerge from what we see.

The intention is not to reproduce the sounds of nature or create literal sound effects. The process begins with a subjective perception of each image, translating its visual qualities into another medium.

The project connects with questions explored in Cazenave’s Soilscapes: how images shape our understanding of nature, how cultural codes influence perception, and whether a landscape exists before it is represented. Through sound, I explore not what these images depict, but what they make us feel.

UNA CREPA NEL PAESAGGIO

A fluid, organic surface meets a rougher mineral texture. The composition follows that transition, introducing irregularities and fragmented layers as continuity gives way to rupture.

Watch Una Crepa nel Paesaggio on YouTube ↗
Can a landscape be experienced differently when we change the language through which we encounter it?

These compositions move from visual perception towards personal interpretation. Rather than establishing a fixed correspondence between image and sound, they explore how changing the language through which we experience a landscape can change the way we perceive it.
